Zoho One and DigiT both try to replace a pile of separate tools with one subscription. They take different approaches, and for many businesses Zoho One will be the better choice. Here is how to decide.

## How they charge

**Zoho One** charges per person. It offers two models: Flexible User pricing, where you license only the users you need, and All Employee pricing, which costs less per person but [requires a licence for every employee on your payroll](https://www.zoho.com/one/pricing/faq.html). At the time of writing, its [US pricing page](https://www.zoho.com/one/pricing/) lists US$37 for All Employee and US$90 for Flexible User on the Standard plan. Prices vary by country, and rupee prices are set on Zoho's India storefront, so check there for your own quote.

**DigiT** charges one flat price for the organization: US$499 a year or US$44.49 a month, with every module and unlimited team members under fair use. Every subscription starts with a 1-month free trial.

So the bigger your team, the more a flat price matters. A per-person price scales with your headcount; DigiT's does not.

## What they cover

**Zoho One** is much broader. Zoho describes it as [50+ integrated apps](https://www.zoho.com/one/tco-calculator.html), and it has been on the market far longer, with a large ecosystem behind it.

**DigiT** is narrower and more focused: CRM, operations, performance, resources, compliance, and customer feedback in a single workspace, with AI analytics and an AI assistant that work across that data.

## When to choose Zoho One

- You want accounting, payroll, or another function from the same vendor.
- You need a long-established product with a large partner network.
- You have a small team, and per-person pricing comes to less than a flat price.

## When to choose DigiT

- You want a smaller, focused product for CRM, operations, and compliance rather than a broad suite.
- Your team is growing and you do not want your bill to grow with every hire.
- You want the whole workspace available in Hindi and Punjabi as well as English.
- You want built-in tools for handling privacy requests under India's [DPDP Act](/blog/dpdp-act-compliance-checklist).

## The simplest way to decide

List the three jobs you most need software to do, then try both products on those jobs. Zoho's pricing and features change over time, so confirm the details on its site before you decide. This post reflects what Zoho's public pages showed in November 2026.
