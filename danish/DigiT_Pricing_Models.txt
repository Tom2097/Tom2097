 DigiT — Subscription Models & Pricing
 Three tiers, priced and scoped to what DigiT actually is · v2.0


 This pricing is built around DigiT's real product — the four workspaces, Smart CRM, and the flagship AI
 Intelligence — not a generic analytics tool. The tiers tell a story: get started (one workspace + AI
 insights), get the brain (the full platform + cross-workspace AI Intelligence), and get everything
 (scale, security, autonomous agents). AI Intelligence is deliberately the spine that makes a customer
 upgrade. Prices are premium-but-accessible for a new AI-native platform, shown in USD for global sale
 with a dedicated, lower India plan in INR.

 Why these prices
 • Premium, but not BI-priced — DigiT is an AI-native operational-intelligence platform, not a dashboard
    tool — so it is priced above commodity analytics, but the $99 entry keeps it accessible and avoids
    anchoring too low (low launch prices calcify and are hard to raise).
 • Professional is the heart — at $399 it bundles the full platform plus the AI Intelligence brain for
    mid-market teams — well below enterprise incumbents while far above commodity tools. This is where
    most customers should land.
 • Enterprise stays custom — scale, security (SSO, DPDP residency), autonomous agents and white-label
    are negotiated — and at the enterprise tier Indian budgets approach Western ones, so there is no need to
    discount.
 • AI Intelligence is the upgrade engine — insights (Starter) → the brain (Professional) → agents +
    simulation (Enterprise). Tiering the flagship feature is what pulls customers up the ladder.


 The India plan — geo-priced, not converted

 Indian B2B buyers pay 50–70% less than Western ones for the same software, so the INR figures are a
 separate, lower plan (billed via Razorpay, gated by location) — not a currency conversion of the USD
 price. That is why Professional is $399 globally but ₹14,999 (~$179) in India. At the enterprise tier the
 gap narrows, because large Indian firms have Western-scale budgets. This updates the earlier
 India-only numbers upward (the original ₹2,999 / ₹11,999 under-priced a product this rich); the new
 India plan is ₹3,499 / ₹14,999 / custom.


 Billing mechanics
 • Annual billing — list prices assume annual (2 months free); monthly billing runs ~20% higher. Annual
    pulls cash forward — valuable for a bootstrapped founder.
 • Seats + AI metering — extra seats beyond the tier cap are an add-on; AI usage above each tier's
    allowance is metered as overage (protects your margin against heavy users).
 • Free trial + founding offer — a 14-day trial, plus a time-boxed “founding customer” discount (30–40%
    off) for the first 10–20 accounts — win early traction without lowering the list price.
 • Dual currency — USD via Stripe internationally, INR via Razorpay domestically; prices exclude 18% GST.


 Deliverable now vs flagged
 Promises to soften before a customer holds you to them.
 • 24/7 phone support → priority support with SLA — you cannot staff a 24/7 phone line solo —
    promise guaranteed response times instead, add 24/7 phone once you have a team.
 • On-premise → “by arrangement / roadmap” — full on-prem on a Next.js + Supabase stack is a major
    lift; offer it by arrangement rather than as a standard checkbox.
 • “Custom AI model training” → “learns your business” — AI Intelligence learning your patterns and
    baselines is real and deliverable; per-customer model fine-tuning is a different, costly promise — word it as
    the former.
 • “Unlimited” → fair-use — keep an internal soft cap so a heavy customer cannot quietly erode margin.
 Note: these are calibrated starting anchors, not fixed prices, and not financial advice. Validate willingness-to-pay with
 your first design partners and adjust.
